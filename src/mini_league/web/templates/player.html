How the rating works

Skill starts at {{ "%.0f"|format(config.mu) }} and moves with every result. Beating someone well above you moves it further than beating someone you were meant to beat. Uncertainty starts at {{ "%.2f"|format(config.sigma) }} and shrinks as you play, so a newcomer swings about and a regular barely moves.

The rating is skill minus {{ "%.0f"|format(config.conservative_k) }} times the uncertainty, times {{ "%.0f"|format(config.display_scale) }}. For {{ player.name }}: ({{ "%.2f"|format(current.mu) }} − {{ "%.0f"|format(config.conservative_k) }} × {{ "%.2f"|format(current.sigma) }}) × {{ "%.0f"|format(config.display_scale) }} = {{ current.rating }}. Subtracting the uncertainty is deliberate: one good morning should not put you top. Only wins and losses count, never the scores.
